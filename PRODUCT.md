# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are people evaluating the author's CV, scholarship applications, or future college applications. Some visitors may be technical readers who want to inspect the quality of the work more closely.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio documents the author's progress as a beginner learning reverse engineering and malware analysis. It should make the current level clear without turning inexperience into the site's main message. Success means a visitor can read the posts, see careful thinking and steady improvement, and recognize future potential.

## Positioning

The portfolio earns credibility through transparent, well-explained learning notes rather than claims of expertise. It presents progress, curiosity, and the ability to communicate technical work clearly.

## Operating Context

Visitors will usually arrive from a CV, scholarship application, or college application. They should be able to understand the site's purpose quickly, scan recent writing, and open a post without needing specialist knowledge. The author will update the site as their knowledge and body of work grow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current focus is documentation and technical write-ups.
- Tools and project downloads may be added later, but they are not part of the present scope.
- The site remains a small multi-page blog with a homepage, post index, individual posts, an about page, and search.
- The displayed name is “nomi.” Account names and contact details remain blank placeholders until the author supplies them.
- The identity mark is a hand-drawn teal vortex. It redraws through closely related imperfect frames only on the homepage; compact placements use a static frame.
- A private authoring studio runs only on the author's computer. It edits versionable content files, supports draft and published posts, and creates a local backup before every save or deletion.
- The authoring studio is not deployed with the public portfolio and requires a local password.
- The interface must remain readable, restrained, responsive, and straightforward to maintain.

## Brand Commitments

- Dark-theme adjacent with onyx, platinum, teal, emerald, and restrained orange available as the established palette.
- Quiet, blog-first presentation with no tactical dashboard styling, status telemetry, aggressive slogans, or exaggerated expertise.
- The Adam Rayyana site is a structural reference, not a visual identity to reproduce.
- The voice should be honest, curious, and precise without sounding apologetic or self-important.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains three sample reverse-engineering and malware-analysis posts.
- Those entries currently function as illustrative portfolio content and are not confirmed as the author's completed work.
- The name “nomi” and the hand-drawn vortex identity are confirmed. No confirmed biography, credentials, tools, awards, testimonials, or production research artifacts are available yet. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Let the clarity of the work demonstrate potential.
2. Be honest about the author's stage without making "beginner" the headline.
3. Make growth visible through dated, well-structured writing.
4. Prefer useful explanation over decorative technical theater.
5. Keep the site simple enough that publishing a new post stays easy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and clear language for visitors who may not have a security background.
